Earth Has Bugs Studio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Earth Has Bugs Studio ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the "Earth Has Bugs" channels (YouTube @EarthHasBugs, its Facebook Page and its TikTok account) to publish that channel's own original animated videos. It is not offered to other users.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google OAuth): the YouTube Data API, to upload videos to the owner's channel, set their titles, descriptions, tags and publish times, and read basic channel information (the channel name and statistics).
  • Facebook (Facebook Login): the owner's Page access token, to upload Reels to the owner's Page and schedule them.
  • TikTok (TikTok Login Kit): the scopes user.info.basic (the account's display name, so the owner can see which account is connected) and video.upload (to send the owner's finished videos to the owner's own TikTok inbox as drafts). The owner reviews, edits and posts each draft in the TikTok app; the app never posts to TikTok by itself.

What is stored

The access and refresh tokens for these accounts, and the connected account names, are stored locally on the owner's own computer only, so uploads can run on a schedule. No data is sent to any server operated by the app, nothing is sold or shared, and no data about other users (viewers, followers or other accounts) is collected.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time: in the app's dashboard ("Disconnect"), and at Google account permissions, Facebook Settings → Business integrations, and TikTok Settings → Security and permissions → Apps and services. Disconnecting deletes the stored tokens.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. Use of Facebook and TikTok data follows the Meta Platform Terms and the TikTok Developer Terms of Service.
